## Why NoDerivatives

The badge is a claim: it says an AI-visibility measurement was actually run. A badge that
anyone may alter and republish cannot carry that claim, because there would be no way to
tell a real one from a redrawn one. ND is not there to restrict you — it is what makes the
badge worth displaying.

## You may

- **Display the badge** on your site, in a README, in a deck, in an app — anywhere — if you
  have actually run an AI-visibility measurement with Clear Cited.
- **Self-host the file** rather than hotlinking it. Keep the link to <https://clearcited.com/>.
- **Change the format or the size.** Converting SVG to PNG, or scaling it, is expressly not
  a modification under section 2(a)(4) of the licence. Scale proportionally.
- **Use the light or dark variant** to suit your background.

## You may not

- **Alter the mark.** No recolouring, restretching, rotating, cropping, outlining, adding
  effects, or changing the wordmark or its spacing.
- **Rebuild the badge** with different text, a different metric, or a different date.
- **Imply a ranking, a score, a certification or an endorsement.** The badge means a
  measurement happened. It does not mean you won anything, and it is not an award.
- **Use the mark as your own**, in your product name, logo, domain, app icon or social
  avatar, or in a way that suggests Clear Cited produced or backs your product.
- **Display it if you have not been measured.**

## Clear space and minimum size

Leave clear space around the badge of at least the height of the "C" in the wordmark.
Do not place it on a busy photograph or a background that reduces contrast below the
variant it was designed for. Minimum width 160 px; below that the wordmark stops being
legible.

## The mark itself

`logo-lockup.svg` is the canonical lockup and is byte-identical to the brand master, which
is deliberate: both badges are generated from it, so the badge cannot drift away from the
brand. If you need the lockup on its own, use that file rather than extracting it from a
badge.

| | |
|---|---|
| Ink | `#0F1720` |
| Brand blue | `#1F6FEB` |
| Wordmark | Inter Bold, outlined — no font needed to render |

All three SVGs are fully outlined, so they render identically on any machine whether or not
Inter is installed.
