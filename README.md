# Measured by Clear Cited — badge

![Measured by Clear Cited](badge-light.svg)

A small "Measured by Clear Cited" badge for products that have run an AI-visibility measurement. Two variants: `badge-dark.svg` and `badge-light.svg`.

A free asset from **[Clear Cited](https://clearcited.com)** — AI search visibility (AEO/GEO) for B2B SaaS and developer tools.

## Embed

Paste this where you want the badge, and it links back to Clear Cited:

```html
<a href="https://clearcited.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Clear-Cited/measured-by-clear-cited/main/badge-dark.svg"
       alt="Measured by Clear Cited" width="220" height="64">
</a>
```

Use `badge-light.svg` on dark backgrounds. You can also self-host the SVG — just keep the link to <https://clearcited.com/>.

## The mark

`logo-lockup.svg` is the canonical Clear Cited lockup: two facing Inter-Bold C’s
with a short bar, plus the wordmark. Both badges are generated from it, so the
badge and the brand cannot drift apart.

| | |
|---|---|
| Ink | `#0F1720` |
| Brand blue | `#1F6FEB` |
| Wordmark | Inter Bold, outlined — no font needed to render |

All three SVGs are fully outlined, so they render identically everywhere and do
not depend on the viewer having a typeface installed. For the full asset set —
favicons, app icons, social avatars and covers — see
<https://clearcited.com/>.

## When to use it

Use it once you've actually measured your AI visibility (e.g. via a free teardown or an audit). It signals you track where AI engines cite you — please don't use it to imply a ranking or endorsement.
